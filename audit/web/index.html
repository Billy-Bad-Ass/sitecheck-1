Questions

What if my site turns out to be fine?

Then the report says so, and lists what's already working. That's a genuine answer, not a wasted one — it means the problem is somewhere other than the website, and you can stop spending money there. Knowing the site is fine is worth having before you pay someone to rebuild it.

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. Every finding is written in plain English and says what it costs you in customers, not what it's called. Each one also has the specific fix, so you can hand the report to whoever maintains your site.

Will you change anything on my site?

No. This is read-only — the site is looked at exactly the way a visitor's browser looks at it. Nothing is altered, and no login is needed.

How thorough is it?

It checks your home page against RULE_COUNT specific things, which is where the problems that affect every visitor live. It is not a full technical audit of every page, and the speed findings are indicators worth investigating rather than a complete performance profile. The report says this too.

How quickly will I get it?
